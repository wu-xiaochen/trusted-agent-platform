## Product promise

**Help enterprises connect existing systems and deliver cross-domain Agent capabilities that people can understand, authorize, and operate.**

Start from one task and a concrete example. The platform guides business owners and solution architects through source capabilities, evidence, rules, tests, a controlled app, and operation. The service contract runs at the business-service layer, so UI, API, Agent, and MCP share authorization and action meaning.

The product does not promise to retrofit every legacy system without supported interfaces or eliminate business-owner/IT participation. Convenience comes from making required work and blockers small and explicit; trust comes from server-side authorization, source-owned commands, durable recovery, and visible evidence.

The initial use case is maintenance preparation and spare-parts request. An employee equipment request is the second-domain test. GitHub Trending top 20 is an aspiration and is not controllable by product design; external reuse and proven customer value are the project goals.
